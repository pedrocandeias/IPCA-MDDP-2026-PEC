# Eligibility Criteria

## Include

- studies on digital prosthetic customization workflows
- studies on interface or interaction design in prosthetic or orthotic configuration systems
- studies involving anthropometric input, 3D scanning, parametric CAD, 3D preview, or clinician-assisted customization
- studies discussing human-in-the-loop decision making, supervised configuration, or collaborative prosthetic fitting
- reviews or empirical papers relevant to user experience, configurator design, or mediated personalization

## Exclude

- papers focused only on low-level control without relevance to configuration or interaction workflow
- papers on unrelated dental prosthodontics unless the interface logic is directly transferable
- papers with no usable connection to digital customization, interface strategy, or user-facing workflow
- purely materials-only papers with no interaction or personalization relevance

## Notes

- Upper-limb prosthetics are preferred.
- Orthotics may be included when the workflow is clearly transferable.
