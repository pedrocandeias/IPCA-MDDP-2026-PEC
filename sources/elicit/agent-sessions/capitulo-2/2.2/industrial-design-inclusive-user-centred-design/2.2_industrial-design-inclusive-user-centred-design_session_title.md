# Session Title

Find peer-reviewed academic literature that discusses the role of industrial design in the development of medical and assistive products, with emphasis on inclusive design and user-centred design approaches. Focus on theoretical frameworks, principles, and methodologies (e.g., inclusive design, universal design, human-centred design) applied to healthcare or assistive devices. Exclude sources that focus primarily on specific fabrication technologies, parametric modelling, or artificial intelligence. Prioritise foundational texts and review papers in design research and design-for-health contexts.
