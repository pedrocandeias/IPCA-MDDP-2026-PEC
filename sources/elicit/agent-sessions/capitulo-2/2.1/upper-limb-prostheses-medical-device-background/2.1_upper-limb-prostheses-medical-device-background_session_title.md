# Session Title

Find peer-reviewed academic literature that establishes the theoretical and clinical background of upper limb prostheses as medical devices. Focus on: definition and purpose of upper limb prostheses; main typologies (passive/cosmetic, body-powered, myoelectric, hybrid); clinical and functional considerations in prosthetic prescription; framing of prostheses within medical device contexts, including regulatory, safety, and clinical validation aspects. Exclude sources primarily focused on industrial design methods, additive manufacturing, digital fabrication, artificial intelligence, or parametric design, as these topics are addressed in later sections. Prioritise review articles and foundational studies published from 2005 onwards.
