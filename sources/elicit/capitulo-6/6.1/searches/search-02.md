# Search 02

## Query

```text
How is AI used as an assistive human-in-the-loop layer rather than a fully autonomous design system in digital workflows for prosthetic or orthotic customization?
```

## Filters

- `minYear`: `2018`
- `type`: `Systematic Review`
- `maxResults`: `10`

## Relevance Assessment

This search was more useful conceptually than the first one, because it pulled literature on machine learning in prosthetics and orthotics and on human-in-the-loop AI. However, it still introduced substantial prosthodontic and dental workflow noise.

## Most Useful Results

1. **Use of machine learning in the field of prosthetics and orthotics: A systematic narrative review** (2023)
   This is the strongest result so far for `6.1`. It helps establish that machine learning in prosthetics and orthotics is already discussed as a support technology for selection, training, monitoring, and decision processes, even if clinical adoption remains limited.

2. **AI-Assisted Data Modelling & Design: A Systematic Literature Review** (2025)
   Not prosthetics-specific, but useful for framing human-in-the-loop patterns such as propose-validate, co-editing, feedback loops, and governance. This may support the conceptual argument that AI in the project acts as a suggestive layer inside a controlled workflow.

3. **Human-in-the-Loop Artificial Intelligence for Asset Management: A Systematic Review...** (2025)
   Domain is not prosthetics, but it may still be useful for language around validation, override, accountability, and interaction between AI recommendations and human operators.

## Less Useful / Noisy Results

- Dental prosthodontics remains a major source of noise.
- Several results focus on restorative dental workflows rather than limb prosthetic customization.

## Implications for 6.1

- We now have stronger support for describing AI as an **assistive and not fully autonomous** layer.
- The best directly relevant source so far is the 2023 review on machine learning in prosthetics and orthotics.
- The chapter can combine:
  - prosthetics-specific literature for domain grounding
  - HITL literature for workflow logic and governance language

## Follow-up

The next search should be even more tightly aligned with the actual system:

- anthropometric data
- parameter suggestion
- clinician/prosthetist oversight
- customization before fabrication
