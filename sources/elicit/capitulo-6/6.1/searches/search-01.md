# Search 01

## Query

```text
What roles can artificial intelligence play in a web-based upper-limb prosthetic customization system where clinicians enter anthropometric data, explicit parametric CAD models generate the geometry, and AI suggests design or fabrication parameters rather than directly generating the prosthetic form?
```

## Filters

- `minYear`: `2018`
- `type`: `Review`
- `maxResults`: `10`

## Relevance Assessment

The search returned partially relevant results. It captured AI in upper-limb prosthetics and AI in CAD/CAM prosthetic workflows, but it also introduced significant noise from dental prosthodontics. For `6.1`, the most useful angle is not autonomous control of bionic hands, but AI as an assistive layer in customization, parameter suggestion, and decision support.

## Most Useful Results

1. **A narrative review on advances in upper limb prosthetics empowered by artificial intelligence...** (2026)
   Useful for broad framing of AI in upper-limb prosthetics, especially as a recent overview. More relevant to control and functionality than to parametric customization, so it should be used carefully.

2. **Artificial Intelligence Techniques for Biosignal Pattern Recognition and Classification in Upper-Limb Prostheses: A Review** (2022)
   Useful for establishing that AI already has a recognized support role in upper-limb prosthetic systems, although focused on biosignal control rather than anthropometric parameter suggestion.

3. **Emerging role of artificial intelligence in the care and management of lower extremity amputations and peripheral nerve injuries** (2025)
   Potentially useful for framing AI in amputation care more broadly, including design and management workflows, but less specific to the present project.

## Less Useful / Noisy Results

- Several results focus on **prosthodontics** and dental CAD/CAM rather than limb prosthetics.
- Some results emphasize **real-time adaptive control** or **robotic grasping**, which are adjacent but not central to the system described in the repository.

## Implications for 6.1

- The chapter should frame AI in this project as a **support layer** rather than as a fully autonomous prosthetic design engine.
- Literature on **AI in prosthetic control** is relevant as contextual evidence that AI already augments prosthetic systems, but it does not directly justify anthropometric-to-parameter mapping.
- The next searches should narrow further toward:
  - anthropometric input to parameter recommendation
  - parametric customization workflows
  - human-in-the-loop decision support
  - secure or mediated AI use in clinical/prosthetic software systems
