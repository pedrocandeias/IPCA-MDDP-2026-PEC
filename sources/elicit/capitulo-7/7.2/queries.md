# Elicit Queries — Capítulo 7.2

## Scope
Experiência do designer/técnico e do utilizador final em plataformas de personalização protésica com parametrização, visualização 3D, supervisão clínica ou técnica, e interação assistida.

## Search Queries

### Search 1 — Designer and technician workflow

What literature discusses the experience of designers, prosthetists, or clinicians using digital platforms for parametric customization of upper-limb prostheses, especially regarding iterative adjustment, comparison of variants, supervision, and decision-making?

### Search 2 — End-user experience and intelligibility

What literature is most relevant to the experience of end users in digital prosthetic customization systems, especially regarding comprehensibility, trust, transparency, perceived control, and participation in the configuration process?

### Search 3 — Shared platforms and differentiated roles

What studies examine digital prosthetic or assistive-device platforms that support differentiated user roles, such as clinicians, technicians, designers, and end users, with different permissions, responsibilities, or levels of control?

### Search 4 — Human-in-the-loop collaboration

What literature is most relevant to human-in-the-loop interaction in prosthetic customization or smart prosthetic systems, where algorithmic assistance, clinician oversight, and user participation coexist in the same workflow?

### Search 5 — Visualization and confidence in configuration

How does 3D visualization, preview feedback, or interactive configuration affect user confidence, usability, and decision quality in prosthetic customization or adjacent medical-device personalization systems?

### Search 6 — Transferable evidence from adjacent domains

What literature from orthotics, rehabilitation devices, assistive technologies, or patient-facing medical customization platforms can inform the comparison between professional and end-user experience in prosthetic configuration systems?

## Report Queries

### Report 1 — Main synthesis for section 7.2

What literature is most relevant to comparing the experience of designers, prosthetists, clinicians, and end users in digital prosthetic customization platforms, especially in systems that combine parametric configuration, 3D visualization, human-in-the-loop assistance, and differentiated permissions or oversight?

### Report 2 — Focus on user experience structure

What does the literature suggest about how digital prosthetic customization interfaces should differ for technical professionals versus end users, particularly regarding interpretability, editable parameters, trust, supervision, and perceived agency?

### Report 3 — Optional bridge to section 7.3

What evidence is most useful for understanding digital prosthetic customization platforms as spaces of asymmetric collaboration between professional expertise and end-user participation, rather than as purely autonomous self-service tools?
