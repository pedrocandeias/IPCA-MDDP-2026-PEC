# Search 03

## Query

```text
How are anthropometric measurements or reference anthropometric profiles translated into prosthetic design parameters, and what role does AI play in improving or accelerating that mapping for upper-limb prosthetic or orthotic customization?
```

## Filters

- `minYear`: `2018`
- `type`: `Review`
- `maxResults`: `12`

## Relevance Assessment

This is the strongest search so far for `6.1`. It still includes prosthodontic noise, but it finally surfaced literature that is much closer to the project’s logic: AI in digital prosthetic design, AI with additive manufacturing, and AI in upper-limb prosthetic review literature.

## Most Useful Results

1. **Mapping the Artificial Intelligence Role in the Design of Digital Prosthetics: A Scoping Review** (2026)
   This appears highly relevant to the chapter because it directly addresses AI in the design of digital prosthetics. It may become one of the anchor references for `6.1`.

2. **When AI meets additive manufacturing: Challenges and emerging opportunities for human-centered products development** (2022)
   Not prosthetics-specific, but very useful for connecting AI, additive manufacturing, and human-centered product development. This can help support broader conceptual framing for the system.

3. **A narrative review on advances in upper limb prosthetics empowered by artificial intelligence...** (2026)
   Useful as a recent contextual review of AI in upper-limb prosthetics, even though its center of gravity is control and functionality rather than parameter suggestion.

4. **AI for Real-Time Prosthetic Adjustments and Adaptations...** (2024)
   Potentially useful for fit, adaptation, and adjustment language. It may also become relevant later for `6.3`.

5. **Artificial Intelligence Techniques for Biosignal Pattern Recognition and Classification in Upper-Limb Prostheses: A Review** (2022)
   Useful as background evidence that AI already operates in support roles inside upper-limb prosthetic systems.

## Less Useful / Noisy Results

- Dental prosthodontics still appears in the result set.
- Some reviews remain stronger on control systems, biosignals, or robotics than on anthropometric-to-parameter mapping.

## Implications for 6.1

- We now have a stronger basis for positioning AI as part of a **digital prosthetic design workflow** rather than only as a control technology.
- The chapter can argue that the proposed system belongs to a broader family of AI-assisted digital customization systems, while still being distinct because it preserves explicit parametric geometry and human oversight.
- The additive manufacturing angle is now better grounded and can help bridge Chapter 6 with earlier discussions on parametric design and fabrication.
