## Core Argument for 6.1

The literature supports three claims that should structure the subsection:

1. AI already has a recognized role in prosthetic systems, but that role is still concentrated mainly in control, biosignal interpretation, and performance adaptation.
2. Parametric modeling and additive manufacturing already provide a workable basis for personalization and geometric customization in upper-limb prosthetics.
3. The integration of anthropometric input, explicit parametric CAD logic, AI-assisted parameter suggestion, and human supervision into one coherent workflow remains underdeveloped, which makes the proposed system relevant.

## Suggested Citation Functions

- Use `Romero et al.` when describing the closest integrated precedent.
- Use `Data-Driven Design Framework` when arguing that intelligent computational methods can support design adaptation and fit optimization.
- Use `Development of Parametric Prostheses...` and `Parametric Design Approach...` when establishing the non-AI technical base of the system.
- Use `Customization of a 3D Printed Prosthetic Finger...` and `Applying 3D Scanning and Printing...` as supporting examples and contextual reinforcement.

## Writing Warning

Do not overstate the literature. The strongest argument is not that fully integrated systems are already established, but that current work remains fragmented and therefore justifies the proposed architecture.
