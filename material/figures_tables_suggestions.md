## Notes on Usage

- Where a paper appears in multiple topic folders with duplicate filenames (e.g. papers with `(2).pdf` variants), the caption content is identical — cite from whichever copy is accessible; the paper reference is the same.
- Figures from the `lower-limb` folder (socket design, transtibial prostheses) are useful for background context on socket fit and digital fabrication workflows, but should be explicitly noted as lower-limb analogies when used in an upper-limb thesis.
- Figures from the `colaboracao` folder are most relevant to Chapters 5–7 (platform, AI integration, interaction) and to section 2.2 (inclusive/participatory design).
- The `amputacao` folder (prevalence, epidemiology) primarily supports section 1.1 — figures from `Global prevalence of traumatic non fatal limb amputation` and `Estimating the Prevalence of Limb Loss in the United States` provide useful epidemiological anchoring for the motivation section.
- The `antropometria` folder is most dense with material relevant to sections 2.4 and 4.2. Priority figures are from: `Biometric analysis hand parameters in young adults`, `2D and 3D anatomical analyses of hand dimensions`, and `Estimation of anthropometric hand measurements using the ratio scaling method`.
