Concluding discussion
Our findings outlined several distinct but intermeshed ways in which HCPs
contribute to medical device development and design within SMEs in the
context of medical industry in a developing country, where the majority of
related activities are geared towards export substitution. Table 1 summarizes
our findings in the form of a framework of roles, responsibilities and objects
of collaboration.
Amongst the roles and responsibilities outlined in this article, designers
we interviewed consider HCPs’ two roles to be particularly critical in design
projects. The first is their role as mediators, where design teams rely on
HCPs’ both knowledge and human resources to enhance their understanding
of the medical issues they focus on. It was not the medical authority the
designers sought in their engagements with HCPs as mediators, but that the
latter can suggest books and articles, provide surgical videos, and grant
